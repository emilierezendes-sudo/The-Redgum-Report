## About the Report

I started The Redgum Report in 2020 when the world was ending--literally. At the time, its purpose was to keep my disbursed family and friends in the loop, and to reassure them we hadn’t become Gollum-like creatures guarding our toilet paper rolls like Sauron’s ring. My precious, indeed. It also gave me a reason to write regularly. 

What began as a piece of paper, haphazardly magnetized to the fridge, slowly expanded into what you see here today. Here you can expect to find family updates, some opinionated book reviews (I love a good book, but hate a bad one), and photos that are 90% nature and 10% my sister.

It comes out roughly bi-monthly, or whenever life allows. I do have a life, after all. ;)
