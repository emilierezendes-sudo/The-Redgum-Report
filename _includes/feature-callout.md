## A New Idea

Up until recently, I never considered my inner musings important or interesting enough to share with anyone other than my grandmother (love you, by the way!), so I honestly surprised myself when I created an email list. I guess I decided I wanted to annoy more people. And just to be clear, I didn’t knock my socks off, but that’s because I like wearing them, instead. 

If you want to subscribe to my email list (hint hint), it will act as an extension of my website, complete with bad jokes (yes, b, not d), personal stories, and any other tidbits that don’t make it onto the official story.
