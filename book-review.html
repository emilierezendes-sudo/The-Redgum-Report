This Month's Author

Anthony Horowitz

Anthony Horowitz is an award-winning, multi-talented, and multi-tasking guy. On a regular basis, he juggles writing books, filming TV series and films, writing plays and being a journalist. He has written over 50 books including the bestselling Alex Rider series, estimated to have sold 21 million copies worldwide and turned into a successful TV series.

The Book We Read

Drop book cover (square)
Alex Rider
Spy Thriller · Book Series

The Alex Rider series is about a 14-year-old English boy by the same name as the books. In the beginning, he is just a regular teenager with a regular life — raised by his uncle Ian Rider since his parents died in a plane crash when he was a toddler. Everything changes when Ian dies in a suspicious car accident, and Alex discovers his uncle wasn't a banker at all, but a spy for MI6 who had secretly been training Alex to be one too.

Alex wants nothing to do with his uncle's business, but is pulled into it again and again by MI6, simply because he looks so inconspicuous he can pass unnoticed. Across the series he survives numerous villains, saves countless lives, and discovers that saving the world is not everything it's cracked up to be.

Hard to Put Down

Recommend

I've really enjoyed the Alex Rider series! It's the kind of book series that is soooo hard to put down — even when I knew I had a ton of homework to get done, I just kept reading. I love the character of Alex: although he hates being dragged into spy business and espionage, he's unwilling to let the bad guys win and is very self-sacrificial and loyal. Those traits, mixed with his natural self-deprecation and awesome skill set, make him a really cool character.

Until next month's stack — happy reading.
